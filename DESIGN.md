## Brand & Style

This design system elevates the portal for NEPA (Núcleo de Extensão e Pesquisa Acadêmica) at Faculdade Católica da Paraíba. Moving away from harsh, saturated splits and heavy contrast blocks, it establishes an academic sanctuary: intellectual, warm, and distinctly contemporary.

### Brand Personality & Core Archetypes
- **The Luminary Scholar:** Grounded in collegiate tradition and scholarly pursuit, radiating trust, structure, and institutional heritage.
- **Warm Collaborative:** Accessible, humane, and welcoming to students, professors, and external researchers.
- **Sophisticated Pastel Aesthetics:** Gentle tinted atmospheric backdrops that feel serene rather than sterile, balancing deep institutional navy with luminous sky tones, soft apricot amber, and subdued coral.

### Design Movement: Modern Academic Warm-Minimalism & Soft Glass
The UI merges soft minimalism with gentle frosted surfaces and subtle tactile card elevations. Glassmorphic accents sit over gentle diffuse gradients inspired by the tri-fold geometry of NEPA's infinity loop, conveying continuity, interdisciplinary research, and community extension.

## Colors

The color palette bridges the solemn dignity of the Catholic University's coat of arms with the vibrant, humanistic energy of NEPA's dynamic triquetra emblem.

### Primary Spectrum (Heritage & Structure)
- **Deep Academic Navy (`#13315C`):** The primary anchor for key actions, deep structural contrast, and prominent typography.
- **Cerulean Mid-Tone (`#2A4E7E`):** Used for active tab borders, hover states on dark elements, and secondary navigational accents.

### Secondary Spectrum (Atmosphere & Trust)
- **Pastel Sky Blue (`#5087C1`):** Gentle supporting shade for icon highlights, focused states, and tertiary interactive cues.
- **Luminous Cloud (`#EBF2FA`):** Used for atmospheric card backgrounds, inner pill badges, and input hover states.

### Warm Accents (NEPA Identity)
- **Soft Coral/Crimson (`#E76F51`):** Derived from the right loop of NEPA's emblem, used for critical indicators, subtle highlights, and joyful accents.
- **Warm Amber Gold (`#E89A3C`):** Honoring the sunburst and fleur-de-lis of the university crest, reserved for badges, status labels, and celebratory markers.

### Background & Surface Hierarchy
- **Canvas Base:** `#F6F8FB` — a velvety, soft-blue off-white creating a gentle canvas that eliminates eyestrain.
- **Surface Elevation 1 (Card):** `#FFFFFF` with 85%–95% opacity blur where layered over brand graphics.
- **Surface Elevation 2 (Elevated):** Pure `#FFFFFF` with soft tinted ambient shadows.

## Typography

The typography leverages **Plus Jakarta Sans** across all hierarchies. Its clean geometry, generous counterspaces, and slight humanist curves bridge academic rigour and digital warmth.

- **Headlines:** Use heavy weights (600 and 700) with slight negative letter tracking (`-0.015em` to `-0.02em`) to provide commanding yet refined structure.
- **Body:** Uses regular (400) weight with relaxed line-heights (`1.5` to `1.6`) for effortless readability across Portuguese academic prose and instructional copy.
- **Labels & Micro-copy:** Rendered with medium-to-semibold weights with subtle positive letter spacing to maintain legibility when set in all-caps or small badges.

## Layout & Spacing

### Desktop 16:9 Split Experience
The desktop layout departs from the unbalanced flat half-split by implementing a harmonious **60/40 visual dialog**:
1. **The Showcase Panel (Left 58%–60%):** A soft, ambient canvas displaying a refined composition of the Catholic University emblem paired with NEPA’s trifold emblem. It features gentle organic aura gradients (sky blue `#DCE9F6` and dawn amber `#FDEED9`), accompanied by inspirational institutional copy and research highlights.
2. **The Authentication Center (Right 40%–42%):** A crisp, elevated soft-card interface nestled with comfortable margins (`margin-desktop`), providing an unhurried, focused login flow.

### Responsive Reflow
- **Tablet (768px – 1024px):** Compresses into an 8-column layout where the institutional panel reduces to an atmospheric hero banner above the authentication card.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** Refits into a single-column fluid card layout. The institutional brand crest sits cohesively at the top as an inline emblem group, with generous `space-md` gaps and thumb-friendly touch targets (minimum 48px).

## Elevation & Depth

This design system avoids heavy drop shadows and high-contrast cutouts in favor of layered luminescent depth:

- **Surface Tier 0 (Canvas Base):** Tinted soft gray-blue (`#F6F8FB`).
- **Surface Tier 1 (Ambient Container):** Translucent frosted glass effect using `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.72)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(20px)` and an ultra-subtle border stroke (`rgba(19, 49, 92, 0.08)`).
- **Surface Tier 2 (Floating Action / Card):** `#FFFFFF` paired with an ambient multi-stage shadow:
  - *Layer 1 (Key light):* `0 2px 4px rgba(19, 49, 92, 0.03)`
  - *Layer 2 (Ambient bloom):* `0 12px 32px rgba(19, 49, 92, 0.06)`
- **Interactive State (Hover / Active):** Y-axis elevation lift of `-2px` with shadow expansion `0 16px 40px rgba(19, 49, 92, 0.10)`.

## Shapes

The shape vocabulary emphasizes friendliness and scholarly approachability without feeling childish:
- **Base Rounding (`rounded`):** `0.5rem` (8px) used for standard form inputs, utility badges, and tooltips.
- **Card & Dialog Rounding (`rounded-lg`):** `1rem` (16px) used for modal cards, outer panels, and segmented control containers.
- **Hero Containers (`rounded-xl`):** `1.5rem` (24px) for the main desktop split-canvas pods and inner hero containers.
- **Interactive Elements (Buttons, Pills):** `0.625rem` (10px) to `9999px` (pill) for status badges and primary actions.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Button:** Deep Academic Navy background (`#13315C`) with crisp white label (`#FFFFFF`). On hover, transitions to Cerulean Mid-Tone (`#2A4E7E`) with an ambient lift. Full width within login forms, height 48px, radius 10px.
- **Secondary / Ghost Button:** Light pastel tint background (`#EBF2FA`) with Navy label (`#13315C`). Zero border or soft border (`rgba(19, 49, 92, 0.1)`).
- **Text / Inline Link:** Cerulean Mid-Tone (`#2A4E7E`) with semi-bold weight. Subtle underline appearance on hover.

### Form Fields & Inputs
- **Container:** Minimum height 48px with generous internal padding (`space-md`).
- **Surface:** `#FFFFFF` background encased in an elegant border: `1.5px solid rgba(19, 49, 92, 0.12)`.
- **Focus State:** Border shifts smoothly to Pastel Sky Blue (`#5087C1`) accompanied by an outer halo glow of `0 0 0 4px rgba(80, 135, 193, 0.15)`.
- **Labels:** Positioned cleanly above the input with `label-md`, rendered in primary navy with an optional subtle pastel badge for required status.
- **Helper / Error State:** Coral Pastel (`#E76F51`) text with matching border stroke.

### Emblems & Co-Branding Display
- **Academic Header Pod:** Both the Catholic University crest and the NEPA triquetra are unified within a subtle frosted glass pill or balanced symmetrical container rather than visually competing. A delicate vertical hairline divider (`rgba(19, 49, 92, 0.1)`) separates them.

### Quick Actions & Social Authentication
- **University SSO Button:** White surface, border `1px solid rgba(19, 49, 92, 0.12)`, containing the institutional seal icon and clear typography: *"Acessar com credenciais acadêmicas"*.
